Airflow & Open-source workflow orchestrator. A pipeline is described as a \textbf{DAG}. \\
DAG & \emph{Directed Acyclic Graph}: a set of tasks linked by dependencies. \\
Sensor & An Airflow task that waits for a condition (e.g., an upstream dbt model to be ready) before downstream tasks run. \\
DBT & \emph{data build tool}: SQL transformation tool. \\
dlt & \emph{data load tool}: a Python \textbf{EL} ingestion library. \\
EL / ETL & Data-integration paradigms: \emph{Extract–Load} (transformation deferred downstream) vs \emph{Extract–Transform–Load}. \\
Snowflake & Cloud data warehouse, the target of every ingestion. \\
S3 & Amazon Web Services object storage, used as a \emph{staging} area. \\
Data contract (Qontract) & A YAML file declaring the fields, types and key of an ingested table. \\
Job Orchestrator & Qonto-internal pattern: a job is declared in a \texttt{schedule.yaml} and its Airflow DAG is \emph{auto-generated}. \\
Kubernetes (K8s) & Container-orchestration platform; each job task runs in an isolated \emph{pod} with its own image and dependencies. \\
PAT & \emph{Personal Access Token}. \\
JWT & \emph{JSON Web Token}: a signed token, used here via a Tableau \emph{Connected App} for authentication. \\
Connected App & A trusted application declared on the Tableau side, enabling signed-JWT authentication instead of PATs. \\
mTLS & \emph{mutual TLS}: authentication in which both client and server present a certificate. \\
PKIoverheid & The Dutch government's public-key infrastructure. \\
Digipoort & The Dutch government's file-exchange gateway (operated by Logius). \\
FTPS & \emph{File Transfer Protocol Secure} (FTP over TLS). \\
CESOP & \emph{Central Electronic System of Payment information}: the EU system for collecting cross-border payment data (VAT anti-fraud). \\
PSP & \emph{Payment Service Provider} (Qonto's status). \\
PR & \emph{Pull Request}: a proposed code change submitted for review. \\
VARIANT & Snowflake's semi-structured type (stores native JSON / arrays / objects). \\
